### UPDATED 12 AUG 2018 BJS


BIT    LOW BYTE VALUE       EFFECT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 0     0x01                 toggle 5.5V RF VCO power
 1     0x02                 toggle 15V RF VCO power
 2     0x04                 toggle PA #1 power
 3     0x08                 toggle PA #2 power

 4     0x10                 toggle RF output switch MSb
 5     0x20                 toggle RF output switch LSb
 6     0x40                 toggle "use VCO" switch (between CW IF VCO and TI digital radio board)
 7     0x80                 toggle PA #3 power


BIT    HIGH BYTE VALUE      EFFECT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 8     0x0100               UNIMPLEMENTED  --  toggle antenna auto-cycling [OR cycle/acrylic/mylar/bare]
 9     0x0200               UNTESTED       --  kill & restart txServer process
10     0x0400               UNTESTED       --  kill & restart cipComm process
11     0x0800               UNTESTED       --  issue full reboot sequence

12     0x1000               toggle TI radio board power
13     0x2000               tune RF VCO down 0.005 GHz
14     0x4000               tune RF VCO up   0.005 GHz
15     0x8000               cycle through pointing modes (lighthouse, GS1, GS2-a, GS2-b, GS2-c, ...)
